PHASE 03 / USAGE → MONETARY CHARGE

Rated costs

Final usage only · Pre-tax usage charges. Open/provisional usage is excluded. These are rated costs, not invoices. Currencies are always reported separately.

Pricing administrator controls

Pricing edits and force re-rating affect future calculations. Retiring configuration does not change existing charges until explicit re-rating. This POC requires trusted access; the actor label is an audit label, not a verified identity.

Force re-rating replaces complete charge sets for usage intersecting these dates across the cloud. Project, resource and currency display filters do not restrict the operation. Old charges remain in the audit history.

Pricing catalog & administration

No production prices are seeded automatically. Create a draft, add rules, activate it, then assign it to a project or the cloud default. Active rules are immutable. Corrections require retiring and replacing configuration, then explicit re-rating.
